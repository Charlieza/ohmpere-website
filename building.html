EV charging for your apartment block

Ohmpere is starting to install charging at apartment blocks and complexes, funded and run by us. Before we propose anything, we want to know two things: how many of your residents would use it, and whether your building can carry it.

  1. Share the residents' survey

    Five minutes, anonymous, and useful even from residents without electric cars.

  2. Tell us about the building

    Ten minutes for a trustee or the managing agent. Rough answers are fine.

  3. Get the results

    We send you the combined results and say plainly whether chargers make sense for your complex.
